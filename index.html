Seungho Jang

I'm a senior undergraduate student at KAIST, double-majoring in Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence. I'm currently a research intern at CVLAB under the supervision of Professor Seungryong Kim.

My research interests include reasoning and planning in language models and multimodal agents. In AgentRVOS, I researched iterative spatio-temporal pruning for visual reasoning over object tracks. In PLAYGROUND, I researched group-wise closed-loop planning with visual feedback for graphic layout generation.
